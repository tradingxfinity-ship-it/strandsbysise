Density is how much hair is on the cap — how full it looks.

Styling & Care

Yes. Because the hair is human, it takes colour beautifully. We do recommend a professional colourist — and know that going darker is always gentler than lifting to blonde, which requires bleach and shortens the lifespan.

Absolutely. Straighten, curl or blow-dry as you would your own hair. Always use a heat protectant and keep your tools below 180°C. Curly textures will return to their pattern after a wash.

  • Wash every 8–10 wears with a sulphate-free shampoo in cool water.
  • Condition mid-length to ends only — never scrub the lace or knots.
  • Air-dry on a wig stand; avoid sleeping in a damp wig.
  • Detangle from the ends upward with a wide-tooth comb.
  • Store in the satin bag your wig arrives in, or on a stand.

Sleeping in it occasionally is fine — braid it loosely and use a satin bonnet or pillowcase. We'd avoid swimming: chlorine and salt water strip moisture quickly. If you do swim, rinse with cool water and deep-condition the same day.
