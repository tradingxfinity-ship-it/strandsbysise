The Details

Raw bouncy is cut from a single donor and finished by hand, with a transparent HD lace, a pre-plucked hairline and bleached knots — ready to wear straight from the box. Choose your colour, length and density to make it yours.

  • 100% virgin human hair, single donor
  • Transparent HD lace with pre-plucked hairline and bleached knots
  • Adjustable elastic band and combs for a secure, glue-free fit
  • Can be dyed, straightened, curled and restyled
  • Average lifespan of 1–2 years with proper care

Treat your wig the way you'd treat your own hair — gently, and less often than you think.

  • Wash every 8–10 wears with a sulphate-free shampoo in cool water.
  • Condition mid-length to ends only; never scrub the lace or knots.
  • Air-dry on a wig stand. Avoid sleeping in a damp wig.
  • Detangle from the ends upward with a wide-tooth comb.
  • Use heat protectant and keep tools under 180°C.
  • Store on a stand or in the satin bag your wig arrives in.

Orders are dispatched within 48 hours, Monday to Saturday. Lagos deliveries arrive in 1–2 working days, and the rest of Nigeria in 2–5. Delivery is complimentary on custom raw units worldwide.

Unworn and uninstalled pieces can be returned within 7 days of delivery in their original packaging. Once lace has been cut, tinted or glued, we're no longer able to accept a return.
