Our story

Beautiful Hair, Made Personal

StrandsBySise began with one belief: a woman should be able to feel like herself on an ordinary Tuesday morning.

How it started

It started with one wig, and one woman who wanted better.

Sise spent years buying hair that looked beautiful in photographs and disappointed in person — thin wefts, stiff textures, lace that never quite disappeared. So she started sourcing her own. First for herself. Then for her sister. Then for the friends who kept asking where she got it.

What began as a favour became a quiet obsession with quality. Today every piece that carries the StrandsBySise name is chosen the same way it was in the beginning: by hand, by feel, and only if it's good enough for Sise to wear herself.
