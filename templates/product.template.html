Treat your wig the way you'd treat your own hair — gently, and less often than you think.

  • Wash every 8–10 wears with a sulphate-free shampoo in cool water.
  • Condition mid-length to ends only; never scrub the lace or knots.
  • Air-dry on a wig stand. Avoid sleeping in a damp wig.
  • Detangle from the ends upward with a wide-tooth comb.
  • Use heat protectant and keep tools under 180°C.
  • Store on a stand or in the satin bag your wig arrives in.
